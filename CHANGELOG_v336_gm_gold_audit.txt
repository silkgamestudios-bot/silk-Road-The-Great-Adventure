Sprite Road v3.36 — GM Gold Audit

- Zona GM > Oro now displays the selected player's server-recorded gold balance.
- Added a master-only server RPC to query balance and to add or subtract gold.
- Every adjustment requires a reason, confirmation, and is recorded in the GM log and server log.
- Anomaly alerts are logged when a player's recorded gold reaches 100,000,000 or more, or an adjustment is at least 10,000,000.
- Player search results now include gold and a visible anomaly marker for high recorded balances.
- The existing positive-only adm.gold operation remains available for compatibility.
