Sprite Road v3.43 — Zone Tuning Editor

- Added a Master-only zone editor to select an individual cave or event.
- Cave settings: mob density (0–4x) and normal loot multiplier (0–5x).
- Event settings: wave density (0–4x), event currency chance (0–100%), and currency multiplier (0–10x).
- Zone settings persist in DB.cfg.zoneTuning and are readable by clients; only the Master can modify them.
- Cave density applies when dungeon floors are generated; existing mobs remain until regenerated.
- Cave loot multiplier applies to normal cave gold, equipment/junk, meat, and material drops that use the existing cave drop pipeline.
- Event density applies to new event waves; event currency settings apply to subsequent kills during that event.
